Slosh

A liquid you can tilt, shake and stir.

  • Tilt your iPad (or drag the tilt pad) to pour it around
  • Shake to make waves
  • Touch with up to five fingers to stir, pour or erase

If your iPad asks for motion access, tap Allow.
Turn on Rotation Lock so the screen doesn’t flip while you tilt.
